# Funding Societies

> Funding Societies (known as Modalku in Indonesia) is Southeast Asia's largest unified SME digital finance platform, founded in 2015 and operating across Singapore, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand and Vietnam. It provides SMEs with business financing (term loans, micro loans, revolving credit lines, invoice and trade financing) and lets retail and institutional investors fund those loans. As of this profile, Funding Societies does not publish a public developer API, SDK, or MCP server.
